Prayer Lock privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026. Applies to the Prayer Lock Android app (com.prayerlock.app).

In short

Prayer Lock has no accounts and no server of its own. What you tell the app and what it learns about your app use stay on your phone. The app sends usage events, crash reports and subscription status to the analytics and billing services named below. It never sends your name, your faith choices, your prayers, your list of apps or your screen time.

What stays on your phone

These are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • the first name you give, your language and your Ishta Devta;
  • the apps you choose to lock, when the lock works, and your reminder times;
  • your japa counts, malas, streak and prayer history;
  • what the app reads through Usage access (see below).

If Android backup is on, Google may back up the app's data to your own Google account. That backup belongs to you; we cannot see it.

Usage access and Display over other apps

With your permission, Prayer Lock uses Android's Usage access to see which app is in front of you, so it can show the prayer screen when you open an app you chose to lock. During setup it also uses Usage access once to show your screen time from yesterday and to suggest the apps you used most. It reads only app names and times. It does this on your phone and never sends the results anywhere.

It uses Display over other apps only to show the prayer screen on top of a locked app.

Prayer Lock does not use an Accessibility service. It never reads your messages, photos, passwords or anything shown on your screen. It does not access your location, contacts, microphone, camera or files.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in your phone's Settings. The lock stops working without them.

What is sent, and to whom

To understand how the app is used, fix crashes, manage your subscription and measure advertising, the app sends the following:

  • Usage events to Google Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel and Meta (Facebook) app events. An event is a fixed name such as "onboarding_completed", "paywall_viewed", "lock_shown" or "jap_done". For a purchase it also includes the product, price and currency.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics to Google Firebase Crashlytics: the technical details of a crash, app version and device model.
  • Subscription status and purchase history to RevenueCat, which tells the app whether your subscription is active.

Each of these services also receives a random ID created when you install the app and the device information it collects itself, including your Android advertising ID, app version, device model, Android version and approximate country (worked out from your internet connection).

Meta uses the events, purchase events and advertising ID to measure which ads led to installs and subscriptions. This counts as sharing data with Meta for advertising. Firebase, Mixpanel, Crashlytics and RevenueCat process data for us as service providers.

We never send your name, your Ishta Devta or any other faith choice, your prayers, your japa counts, the apps you lock or use, or your screen time. Because Prayer Lock is a prayer app, though, an event by itself does tell these companies that you use a devotional app.

All of this is sent encrypted (HTTPS). The app shows no ads and we do not sell data.

Advertising ID

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads (on some phones: Google › Ads). New events will then not be linked to the old ID.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and charged by Google Play. We never see your card, UPI or bank details. Cancel in Play Store › your profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions.

Children

Prayer Lock is meant for adults (18 and over). It is not designed for children and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Deleting your data

On your phone: open Settings › Apps › Prayer Lock › Storage › Clear storage, or uninstall Prayer Lock. This deletes everything the app stored: your name, choices, japa counts, streak and settings. We hold no copy, because none of it is sent to us.

Events, crash reports and subscription records: the services above keep these, linked only to the random install ID and your advertising ID, for their standard periods (Firebase Analytics up to 14 months, Crashlytics 90 days, Mixpanel up to 5 years by default, Meta and RevenueCat under their own policies). To have them deleted, email the developer address on Prayer Lock's Google Play page and include the Feedback ID from the app's Help screen if you have one. We will ask each service to delete the data for that ID within 30 days.

Purchases: Google Play keeps its own purchase records under Google's privacy policy.

Changes

If we change what the app collects or sends, we will update this page and the date at the top before the new version is released.

Contact and grievances

For any question, request or complaint about your data, email the developer address shown on Prayer Lock's Google Play page. We reply within 7 days.
