Prayer Lock support

Help for the Android app

The prayer screen doesn't appear

Open Prayer Lock › Settings and check the lock is on and the "Prayer Lock is on" notification is showing. Then check, in your phone's Settings:

  • Usage access: Settings › Apps › Special app access › Usage access › Prayer Lock › On.
  • Display over other apps: Settings › Apps › Special app access › Display over other apps › Prayer Lock › On.
  • Battery: Settings › Apps › Prayer Lock › Battery › Unrestricted (or "No restrictions").

The lock stops after a while (Xiaomi, Redmi, Poco, Vivo, iQOO, Oppo, Realme, OnePlus)

These phones close apps in the background to save battery. Two settings fix most cases:

  • Autostart: on Xiaomi, Redmi and Poco: Security app › Permissions › Autostart › Prayer Lock › On. On Vivo and iQOO: i Manager › App manager › Autostart. On Oppo and Realme: Settings › Apps › Prayer Lock › Battery usage › Allow auto launch and Allow background activity.
  • Keep in recents: open the recent apps screen, then long-press (or swipe down on) Prayer Lock and tap the lock icon.
  • Xiaomi only: Settings › Apps › Prayer Lock › Other permissions › "Display pop-up windows while running in the background" › Allow.

Menu names change between phone models and updates. If you can't find one, email us your phone model and we'll send the exact steps.

Reminders are not showing

Allow notifications for Prayer Lock in your phone's Settings, then set your morning and sandhya times in Prayer Lock › Settings. The battery and autostart steps above also help reminders arrive on time.

Turn the lock off, or remove the app

Switch the lock off any time in Prayer Lock › Settings. You can also uninstall Prayer Lock normally; it never blocks uninstalling. Uninstalling does not cancel your subscription (see below).

Restore your subscription

On a new phone, sign in with the Google account you paid with, then open Prayer Lock › Settings › Restore purchase.

Cancel or change your subscription

Play Store › your profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Prayer Lock. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Refunds go through Google Play's refund policy.

A mistake in a prayer or translation

Please email us the prayer, the language and what is wrong. We read every message.

Your data

Your name, choices, japa counts and app use stay on your phone. See the privacy policy for what is sent and how to delete it, and the terms of use.

Contact

Email the developer address shown on Prayer Lock's Google Play page, or use Prayer Lock › Help › Contact us inside the app. We reply within 2 working days.
