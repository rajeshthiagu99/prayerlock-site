2. What the app does

Prayer Lock lets you choose apps that open only after you finish a short naam jap or prayer. It is a personal tool for building a habit. You set it up yourself and you can switch it off or uninstall it at any time.

The lock depends on Android permissions you grant (Usage access, Display over other apps, battery and autostart settings, notifications) and on your phone allowing the app to keep running. Some phones stop background apps on their own. We work to keep the lock reliable but cannot promise it will catch every app opening on every phone.
